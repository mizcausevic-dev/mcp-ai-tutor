# Why mcp-ai-tutor exists

AI Tutor Cards are JSON disclosures. This MCP server makes six common review tasks available to a compatible client: construct a discovery URL, fetch or validate a card, summarize its fields, compare declared subject coverage, and check the draft's under-13/COPPA field rule.

The tools can help a reviewer organize questions for a vendor. They do not test a tutor with learners, verify its privacy controls, or determine legal compliance. Remote cards are publisher content and must be treated as untrusted. The server is a local stdio utility; it does not provide a hosted procurement service or a live policy engine.
